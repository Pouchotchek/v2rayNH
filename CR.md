# v2rayNH privacy information

v2rayNH stores profiles, subscription URLs, and settings on your device. Exported backups can contain connection credentials.

When a subscription is refreshed, the app contacts its provider. A latency test contacts the configured test endpoint through the selected connection. The manual test can also look up the connection's public IP and country. Automatic latency checks do not perform that lookup.

The update screen contacts the v2rayNH GitHub repository. Downloading routing data or opening a link can contact the service that hosts it. Those services handle requests under their own privacy practices.

Questions about v2rayNH can be sent through [the fork's issue tracker](https://github.com/Pouchotchek/v2rayNH/issues). Avoid posting subscription URLs, credentials, or unredacted logs in public issues.
